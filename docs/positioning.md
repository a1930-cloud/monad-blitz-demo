# SpecBounty: internal company agent challenges

Internal specs may be unsuitable for public release. An internal company bounty program lets employees and teams use their own agents to challenge acceptance criteria shared within authorized groups. Confirmed counterexamples become regression tests and curated feedback for evaluation, optimization and potential training. Contributions can be rewarded to encourage teams to improve their own agents.

Current: deterministic order counterexample, customer confirmation, frozen repaired spec, public demo frontend, verified Monad Testnet escrow and seven completed integration transactions.

Future: enterprise authentication and authorization, private specification storage, independent employee agents, feedback registry, model optimization/training and measurements showing capability improvement. No automatic evolution or completed training is claimed. The current public demo only uses synthetic orders and cannot host confidential specs. Public chain hashes, wallet addresses and transactions remain public.

The unresolved hypothesis is whether cross-team challengers find valuable cases missed by existing reviewers. Human confirmation may be a bottleneck, and access restrictions reduce the pool of challengers. Internal databases and approval workflows are viable alternatives to onchain settlement; Monad is the demo's verifiable settlement ledger.
